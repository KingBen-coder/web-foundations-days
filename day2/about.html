About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture ideas, reminders, and important thoughts before they disappear. It helps you keep your notes organized and easy to access while you work or study.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the QuickNotes home page.
  2. Type your idea or reminder into the new note box.
  3. Click the Add Note button to add the note to your list.
  4. Review your saved notes whenever you need them.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas and reminders.
  • Simple and easy-to-understand interface.
  • Organized list of your notes.
  • Keyboard-friendly controls and accessible HTML structure.
